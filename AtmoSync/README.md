# AtmoSync: Micro-Climate Arbitrage Analytics

## Project Overview

AtmoSync is a supply chain analytics platform designed to monitor
micro-climate conditions inside shipping containers.

The platform analyzes temperature, humidity, vibration and other
environmental conditions to identify spoilage risk, remaining shelf life,
and potential Spoilage Arbitrage opportunities.

## Problem Statement

Traditional supply chain analytics mainly rely on standard transit times
and macro-level weather information. They may not detect sudden
micro-climate changes inside individual shipping containers.

AtmoSync aims to detect these changes and provide analytics that can
support faster supply chain decisions.

## Main Objectives

1. Monitor container environmental conditions.
2. Detect abnormal temperature and humidity.
3. Analyze spoilage risk.
4. Estimate remaining shelf life.
5. Calculate Spoilage Arbitrage.
6. Identify at-risk containers.
7. Visualize results through a dashboard.
