# Risk & Spoilage Analysis – KPI Documentation

## 1. At-Risk Shipments
- Counts shipments with At Risk status.
- Identifies shipments that may face spoilage.
- Helps monitor shipments requiring attention.

## 2. Spoiled Shipments
- Counts shipments marked as Spoiled.
- Shows the number of already spoiled shipments.
- Helps track shipment spoilage.

## 3. Average Spoilage Risk
- Calculates the average spoilage risk percentage.
- Uses the spoilage_risk_percent column.
- Helps understand the overall spoilage risk level.

## 4. Average Exposure Hours
- Calculates the average exposure duration in hours.
- Uses the exposure_duration_hours column.
- Helps understand how long shipments were exposed to environmental conditions.

## Purpose
- To monitor shipment risk and spoilage.
- To identify shipments requiring attention.
- To support shipment risk analysis in Power BI.
