# AtmoSync Dashboard KPI Plan

## Selected KPIs

1. At-Risk Shipments
   - Count of shipments with "At Risk" spoilage status.

2. Spoiled Shipments
   - Count of shipments with "Spoiled" status.

3. Average Spoilage Risk
   - Average value of spoilage risk percentage.

4. Average Exposure Hours
   - Average exposure duration of shipments.

These KPIs were selected to monitor shipment risk, spoilage,
and exposure conditions.
